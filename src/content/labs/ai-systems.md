K2Labs' AI Systems Lab focuses on building robust, efficient AI systems that work under real-world constraints: limited compute, intermittent connectivity, and minimal data labeling.

We prototype systems that prioritize safety, interpretability, and real-world utility — then test them with partner communities to ensure they solve the right problems.
